SLEEPLESS GIRL - 32x32 Horror Pixel Art Character
by chuckyassets - https://chuckyassets.itch.io

A small girl in a nightgown, clutching her teddy bear.
Made for horror, survival and atmospheric platformer games.

SPECS
- Frame size: 32x32 px
- Pivot: bottom-center (x=16, y=31), feet rest on row y=30
- Suggested hitbox: x=12, y=10, w=10, h=21
- 7 animations, 35 frames, facing right and left

ANIMATION   FRAMES   FPS   LOOP
idle        4        4     yes
walk        6        8     yes
run         6        12    yes
hurt        3        10    no
fear        4        8     yes
attack      5        12    no
death       7        8     no

FILES
protagonist_sheet.png
    Every animation in one 224x224 sheet: 7 columns x 7 rows,
    one animation per row in the order above, facing right.
protagonist_sheet.json
    Frame size, pivot, hitbox, row index, frame count, fps and loop
    flag for each animation.
protagonist_<animation>_right.png / protagonist_<animation>_left.png
    One horizontal strip per animation and direction.
